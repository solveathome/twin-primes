the original tile, and it produced eight new proven lemmas, a complete exact

## 1. What is now PROVEN that was not this morning

**Level Ledger.** For every residue a mod p, |h(a) − D/p| ≤ 2·3^{π(x)−1}, so every
copy of a fold loses 2D/p ± 4·3^{π(x)−1} slots, uniformly in k and p. The relative
error is exp(−x(1+o(1))); the bound crosses below the mean at x = 19 and reads
0.048 of it at T₂₃. The counting half of the fold question is closed
unconditionally.

**Mirror Ledger.** K(k) = K(p−1−k) to within exactly 1, the defect caused by the
single slot at W−1 whose representative does not shift under r ↦ W−2−r. VERIFIED
45 of 45 cells, always exactly two mismatched residues with values +1 and −1 at
the two predicted addresses.

**Head Lemma.** On the fold ladder, copy 0's kills below p² lie in {p, p²−2}.
The first is a self-strike (a twin FOUND), the second is the leading edge of the
stratum. Sharpens A6's head-calm audit.

**Impact Lemma (Chris's, in exact form).** Sifting [0,W) in increasing order of
primes, a removal at r = p·t needs t rough below p, so t = 1 or t ≥ p, so r lies
in {p−2, p} or r ≥ p²−2. Hence past p² > W+2 every removal is a graduation, and
the number of folds that can do genuine damage is finite and explicit,
π(√(W+2)) − π(x). VERIFIED exhaustively: 11-tile 64 folds / 64 removals / 0
violations / 0 non-twins; 13-tile 456/456/0/0.

**Second narrowing.** A composite p-rough cofactor is at least p², so r ≥ p³, so
past p > W^{1/3} every removal sits at a prime or a semiprime. VERIFIED, zero
exceptions in 7,056,385 removals.

**Head monotonicity.** A position r < p² is a slot of T_p only if it was a slot at
every earlier level. The head is pure attrition; nothing is ever created there.

**Natal Dispersion Lemma (Chris's misalignment intuition, in exact form).** For
q > p, gcd(W,q)=1 and q > p−1 make k ↦ kW−1 injective mod q on [1,p−1], so the
fold by q removes at most two members of natal@p. VERIFIED, never exceeded.

**The lineage identity.** rarity (y−3)/y# × quality m̄(y) = (y−3)/D_y = the share,
EXACT at every level.

## 3. What is CLOSED, and why each closure was forced

- **"Fold further and count."** The survival law is a function of u = ln W / ln y
  and of nothing else, so a bigger tile moves W and the target together and buys
  nothing. The tile is a picture of the question, not a lever on it.
- **The natal creation engine.** The natal term is a rounding error in the fold
  law (4.5e−9 at fold 31); the cohort's expected twin content is 3.17 ln²x / x → 0,
  so it is asymptotically EMPTY; and the Dispersion Lemma protects it only out to
  u ≈ x/ln x, which is far above β₂ = 4.26645 and therefore already covered by the
  standard sieve for free.
- **A favourable lineage or window.** The Hardy-Littlewood singular series for the
  pair {0,2} is constant, so all D_x admissible classes carry equal density. The
  tile's whole anatomy is residue-based, so it could not have distinguished
  anything. Both negatives were forced.
- **The sub-binomial dispersion as an anomaly.** It is real, but the
  tile-normalized statistic is the Poisson Fano divided by (1−p) *by algebra*. It
  is the ordinary short-interval variance of twin primes, classical and expected.
- **The pane, a second time.** Chris's point that overlapping windows cost nothing
  for an existence proof removes the pane's original motivation, and A7 had
  already shown it strictly harder than the zone.

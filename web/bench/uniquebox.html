The stricter reading: n is a k-box number when there is exactly one unordered set of k factors, each at least 2, whose product is n. That holds precisely when Ω(n) = k, plus the single exceptional family p^(k+1). So every hole boxes uniquely at one k, and the prime-power holes box uniquely at two.
